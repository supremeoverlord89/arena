Controls

Pilot

You earn 3 points every level, 300 by level 100. Firepower and Hull grow the same amount with every point. The other stats have caps, so each point there is worth a little less than the last. Ship parts add points on top, shown in green.

Respec is free for now.

Loadout

Pick a ship, then fill Q, E, R and two passives. Click a slot and then an ability, or drag abilities onto slots.

Actives

Passives

Ion does 30% more to slowed targets. Cryo slows. Plasma burns over time. Kinetic knocks back.

Sectors

Each sector is three waves of enemy ships and then the region's boss. Clearing one opens the next. Every five sectors you fly into a new region with its own enemies, dangers and boss. Bosses sometimes drop abilities and blueprint fragments for their own ship.

Hangar

Every slot has a main stat, shown in bold on its parts: Main Gun and Targeting → Firepower, Hull Plating → Hull, Cockpit → Shields, Thrusters → Thrust, Reactor Core → Overclock. Hull Plating can add Repair and Thrusters can add Maneuvering. Modules can roll anything.

Achievements

Milestones pay out credits as soon as you reach them, and a few unlock ships.

Shop

Auction House

Buy ship parts other pilots have listed, or list your own from the Sell parts tab. Listings last 48 hours. The house keeps 10% of each sale, and your credits arrive even if you're offline.

Settings

Click an action, then press the key or mouse button you want. Esc cancels. Key settings are saved on this computer.
